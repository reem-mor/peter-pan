# INC-2307 — 5xx storm from bad canary (2026-06-03)

**Severity:** P1 · **Duration:** 22 min · **Class:** availability

**What happened:** a canary carrying a broken migration took 10% of traffic
and returned 5xx on every write. Error-rate alert fired at T+2m; rollback
restored availability at T+6m after approval.

**Detection:** availability alert. **Resolution:** canary rollback.

**Lessons:**
- Rollback was fast *because* it was a one-click approved action — the
  approval gate did not slow response, it made the action auditable.
- Canary analysis now requires write-path checks, not just read-path.
