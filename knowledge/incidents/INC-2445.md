# INC-2445 — disk full on the logging node halted ingestion (2026-07-08)

**Severity:** P2 · **Duration:** 38 min · **Class:** resource_exhaustion

**What happened:** the primary logging node hit 100% disk at 21:30 and stopped
accepting writes, so log ingestion for the whole cluster stalled and several
services blocked on synchronous log flushes. The root cause was a log-rotation
job that had silently stopped running after a debug flag left DEBUG-level
logging enabled on a chatty service, tripling daily log volume and outpacing the
rotation window.

**Detection:** disk-usage alert at 90% (T+0, but it had been climbing for hours
under a too-high threshold) and a log-ingestion-stalled alert. **Resolution:**
compressed and archived old segments to free space, disabled the stray DEBUG
flag, and restarted the rotation job (T+35m). Added a volume-expansion follow-up.

**Lessons:**
- Move the disk warning threshold to 80% with rate-of-change, so a fast fill is
  caught with time to act rather than at the wall.
- Alert when the rotation job hasn't completed on schedule — a stuck rotator is
  the usual root cause, per the disk-pressure runbook.
- Never let synchronous logging block request threads; degrade to drop-and-count.
