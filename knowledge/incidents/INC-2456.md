# INC-2456 — internal DNS resolution failure after policy change (2026-07-14)

**Severity:** P1 · **Duration:** 23 min · **Class:** connectivity

**What happened:** services began failing with host-not-found errors between
namespaces at 11:47, immediately after a network-policy change tightened egress
rules. The change accidentally blocked UDP/53 to the in-cluster DNS resolver, so
every new name lookup timed out while existing connections (already resolved)
kept working — which made the blast radius look random until the pattern was
clear. Checkout and search, which open fresh upstream connections per request,
were hit hardest.

**Detection:** connection-error alerts across multiple services (T+2m); the
change-timeline flagged the network-policy deploy in the same window.
**Resolution:** reverted the egress policy to restore UDP/53 to the resolver
(T+20m); resolution recovered immediately and error rates cleared.

**Lessons:**
- Distinguish refused (target down) from timeout (path/policy) first — the DNS
  runbook's step 1 saved triage time here.
- Network-policy changes now carry an explicit allow-rule for cluster DNS and a
  from-inside-the-namespace resolution smoke test in the deploy pipeline.
- Resolve-by-name from the affected namespace is the fastest confirming probe.
